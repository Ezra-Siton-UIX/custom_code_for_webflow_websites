INCOSE IL 2027 – Workshop Day (Jan 13, Online)

The second day of INCOSE IL 2027 takes systems engineering into practice. Workshop Day is designed for professionals who want to deepen their skills, hold in-depth discussions with international experts, explore methods they can apply immediately in their own projects, and find inspiration for research and innovation.

This year’s program brings together leading voices from the United States and Israel for a full day of interactive, hands-on sessions:

  • Prof. A – Tutorial A
  • Dr. Yaniv Mordecai (Tel Aviv University & Amazon) – Tutorial B
  • Ms. C – Tutorial C
  • Dr. D – Tutorial D
  • Prof. E – Tutorial E

By joining Workshop Day, you’ll gain

  • Practical experience with methods and tools in smaller, interactive groups.
  • Cross-industry insights bridging defense, civil, and hi-tech domains.
  • Actionable outcomes to strengthen your own projects and organizations.

Day 1 inspires with ideas. Day 2 equips you with the skills, strategies, and confidence to act on them. This day is virtual, allowing for the opportunity to host leading experts from the US, making it easier for attendees to take notes and practice.

Good Luck!
